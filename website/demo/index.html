Before you begin

A quiet room and a few minutes of stillness make the biggest difference.

  • Choose a time when you won't be interrupted.
  • Let your breathing slow down before the music starts.
  • Find a comfortable seat. You don't need an account, an app or any experience.
  • Give the music a few minutes before you judge it.
  • Click in each sound to change it to your liking.
Your browser needs one tap before it can make sound.

The four people are fictional characters, and their stories are invented for this demo. Nothing here is medical or therapeutic advice.
