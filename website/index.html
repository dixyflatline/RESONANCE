This is not therapy. It is a space to breathe, settle and listen inward. The journey is yours.

About the performer

Who is in the room with you.

Wei Gao builds live music for people who want to slow down and listen.

Wei co-founded MetaOrigin, which builds AI tools for music education, and has spent years exploring how people learn to listen.

RESONANCE started from a simple question: what happens to a room when the people in it help shape the music?

What I have found out, surprisingly, is that people don't just express themselves. They need to collaborate, compromise, and experiment. It is like a metaphor for exploring life and finding beauty in it.

How a session unfolds

From settling in to gently returning.

  1. 1

    Arrive and settle

    The room is quiet and the music begins softly. Nothing is asked of you yet. Simply arrive as you are.

  2. 2

    Find your rhythm

    Scan a QR code to participate in the music creation. Or wear an EEG to control the music with your mind. In either case, settle into a calm, invigorating state of mind.

  3. 3

    Go deeper, or come back

    The music moves with the mood of the room, opening when people are ready and softening when they need stillness.

  4. 4

    Gently return

    The session ends with one soft bell-like sound, an invitation to return to the room, or to yourself, at your own pace.

You in the room

Your experience, your pace.

Join in as much or as little as you like. Nothing is required of you, and nobody is put on the spot.

  • Come and go as you wish. Stepping out for a moment is always fine.No explanation is needed.
  • Sit, stand or lie down. The music adapts to the space you are in.Eyes open or closed, whichever feels right.
  • Choose what you focus on. There is no score and no wrong way to listen.Let your attention wander, and gently bring it back when you notice.
